# Privacy Policy

**VGC Battle Notes**
Last updated: May 21, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account data**: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name to identify your account and enable cloud sync across your devices.

**User content**: Battle notes, team builds, and matchup analyses you create are stored on our servers to provide the core functionality of the app.

**Usage data**: We collect anonymous crash reports and performance metrics (via Firebase Crashlytics) to improve app stability. This data is not linked to your identity.

**Sign-in providers**: If you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, we receive only the information those providers share (typically name and email) to create your account.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To create and maintain your account
- To sync your teams and battle notes across devices
- To provide AI-powered matchup analysis (Premium)
- To diagnose and fix crashes and performance issues

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Auth), governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from **Settings → Delete Account**. Deletion is irreversible and removes all your data from our servers within 30 days.

## 5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can delete it.

## 6. Third-Party Services

- **Firebase (Google)** — authentication, database, crash reporting
- **Apple StoreKit** — subscription management
- **Google Sign-In** — optional sign-in method
- **Anthropic / Groq** — AI matchup analysis (Premium feature; your team data is sent to generate analysis and is not retained)

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at: **support@vgcbattlenotes.app**
